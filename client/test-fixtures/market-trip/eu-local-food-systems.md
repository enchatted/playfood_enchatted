# EU Local Food Systems

Publisher: European Commission, EUR-Lex

URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52013DC0866

## Verified public passages

> There is no uniform definition of the term ‘local area’. While various sources confirm that this term means a relatively small geographical area, there is no agreement on the distance, varying between 20 and 100 km from the point of production. Taking into account the wide-spread interpretations of the term ‘local area’, a definition at EU level would appear arbitrary.
